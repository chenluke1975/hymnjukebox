<p>A big-button hymn jukebox. Everything comes from and is streamed directly from the official websites of <b>Taiwan Gospel Book Room</b>; the app bundles no audio, lyrics or scores.</p>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Number pad</b>: pick Hymns / Supplement / Children, type a number and open the official mygoodland.video accompaniment page at that hymn. mygoodland.video is a paid subscription service; sign in with your own account to play.</li>
<li><b>Previews</b>: free official online previews of TWGBR hymn albums, with a queue and background playback.</li>
<li><b>Title search</b> over albums, tracks and the hymn titles shown on the official pages.</li>
<li><b>Favorites</b> and <b>Recent</b>; notification and headset controls.</li>
<li>Large text and big buttons.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Disclaimer</b>: independent open-source software, not affiliated with or endorsed by Taiwan Gospel Book Room, which owns the hymns. No ads, no tracking, no data collection.</p>
